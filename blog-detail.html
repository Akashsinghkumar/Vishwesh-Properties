Why Kisan Path & Sultanpur Highway Are Lucknow's Fastest Growing Real Estate Hubs

  • Ankit Mishra
  • Investment Corridors
  • October 2026

The Sultanpur Road and Kisan Path (Outer Ring Road) corridors have emerged as Lucknow’s most lucrative real estate investment belts. Backed by extensive infrastructure developments, 6-lane express connectivity, commercial hubs, and LDA-governed expansion, this belt offers unprecedented capital appreciation potential for homebuyers and investors.

1. Strategic Connectivity & Infrastructure Advantage

Kisan Path (Lucknow Outer Ring Road NH-230) provides seamless signal-free transit between Sultanpur Highway, Ayodhya Road, Raebareli Road, and Kanpur Road. Commute times to CCS International Airport and Charbagh Railway Station have been reduced to under 30 minutes, attracting premium gated residential townships and logistics players alike.

Proximity to key healthcare and entertainment milestones—including Medanta Super Specialty Hospital, Ekana International Cricket Stadium, and Phoenix Palassio—makes this corridor prime territory for both personal residency and high rental returns.

“Kisan Path connects all 10 national and state highways intersecting Lucknow, making properties along this ring road the top pick for residential societies and commercial logistics.”
Vishwas Property Advisory & Research Team
2. What Property Types Offer the Best Returns?

Residential Gated Plots: High buyer preference for immediate possession plots with 30ft to 40ft wide roads, underground electrification, and immediate registry/mutation (Dakhil Kharij). Prices along Kisan Path have grown over 35-45% over the past two years.

Duplex Luxury Villas: Families looking for independent homes in secure gated communities are investing in ready-to-move and under-construction villas near Sultanpur Highway and Jalsa Resort.

Commercial & Warehouse Land: The direct highway accessibility has turned this stretch into a warehousing and commercial showroom hub, guaranteeing robust long-term rental yields for commercial investors.
